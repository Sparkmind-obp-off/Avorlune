# 55 — Service-Led Semi-Marketplace Product Strategy

**Status:** Approved product direction.  
**Business:** AVORLUNE — AI Creative Production.

## Decision

AVORLUNE should feel familiar to customers of a specialist freelance-service marketplace: discover a service, configure needs, submit a brief, receive a quotation, approve scope, pay, follow production, review previews, and receive deliverables.

It is **not** an open freelancer marketplace. AVORLUNE does not recruit or assign independent freelancers as its fulfilment model. AVORLUNE itself fulfils the service using authorized AI tools (initially Genspark), creative direction, human selection/editing, quality review, and accountable operations.

**Customers buy creative production services and agreed deliverables—not AI credits, Genspark account access, subscriptions, or model access.**

## Positioning

Customer-facing category: AI Creative Production.

Describe AVORLUNE as a creative production service for brands, businesses, and creators that need AI-assisted image/video content with human direction and review.

Do not position it as:
- An open platform for hiring individual freelancers.
- A reseller of AI credits, accounts, or subscriptions.
- A generic AI model/API provider.
- An autonomous AI service that guarantees exact outputs without review.

## Marketplace-like capabilities

Adopt:
- Curated service catalog and categories.
- Service detail pages, deliverables, options, and add-ons.
- Structured brief builder and saved drafts.
- Customer quotation and scope approval.
- Order/payment tracking.
- Production timeline and notifications.
- Preview, revision, approval, and private delivery.
- Support, cancellation, and refund records.

Do not adopt seller registration, freelancer bidding, public worker profiles, peer-to-peer payouts, or escrow claims.

## Two purchase paths

### Ready-to-order
A standardized, clearly scoped service with defined inputs, outputs, quantities, formats, revision allowance, and timing. Price may be fixed only after costs and capacity are validated. Full upfront payment is acceptable only when the complete scope and terms are visible before checkout.

### Custom project
Free brief → AVORLUNE review → quotation → customer approval → 50% deposit → production → preview/revisions → 50% balance → final delivery.

A submitted brief is an inquiry, not an accepted order or reserved production slot.

## Human-in-the-loop

Humans remain responsible for brief interpretation, creative direction, source-rights review, output selection/refinement, quality assurance, customer communication, and final approval. An AI generation result is not automatically a deliverable.

## Unit economics

Quote based on outcomes and scope, considering provider usage, human production time, number of concepts/variants, revision allowance, licensed assets, payment fees, overhead, risk, and margin. Track actual cost per project. Do not sell a customer credit balance.

## Initial operating model

Start with one AVORLUNE-operated production team and a curated catalog. Validate demand, quality, delivery times, actual margin, and repeat purchases before adding more providers or automation.

## Success metrics

Track brief completion, brief-to-quote time, quote acceptance, deposit conversion, on-time delivery, first-preview approval, revision count, refunds/cancellations, contribution margin, repeat purchase, and customer satisfaction.
