# 03 — North Star & Metrics

## North Star Metric
**Monthly Approved Paid Deliverables (MAPD):** count of customer deliverable sets that are both paid (or manually payment-confirmed) and explicitly approved/delivered in the month.

This is more meaningful than raw generations, prompts, signups or views because it ties creative output to customer value and payment.

## Supporting metrics
### Demand
- Qualified inquiries per week
- Inquiry-to-quote rate
- Quote acceptance rate
- Paid pilot conversion rate

### Production
- Median time from accepted scope to first preview
- On-time delivery rate
- Average revision rounds per project
- Producer hours per approved deliverable set
- Rework rate

### Quality and customer value
- Approval on first preview
- Customer satisfaction after delivery
- Repeat purchase rate
- Referral-sourced paid projects

### Economics
- Revenue per project
- Direct tool and production cost per project
- Gross contribution per project
- Refund/chargeback rate

## Guardrails
- Do not optimize for generation volume at the expense of usable quality.
- Track tool usage and cost per job.
- Never count unpaid demo work as paid demand.
- Distinguish customer approval from internal completion.
- Do not claim campaign performance without access to reliable campaign data.

## Initial operating targets
Targets should be set only after the first 5–10 real projects. Before that, collect baseline data rather than inventing conversion benchmarks.
