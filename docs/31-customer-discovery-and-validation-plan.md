# Customer Discovery and Validation Plan

> **PLAN / HYPOTHESES — no interviews or pilots have been recorded as completed.**

**Status:** Prepared; execution pending.  
**Purpose:** Validate whether AVORLUNE's AI image/video production offer solves a real, paid customer problem before spending on infrastructure or scraping.

## 1. Validation principle
Likes, views, survey enthusiasm, and AI-tool curiosity are not purchase demand. Strong evidence is a qualified buyer describing a current need, accepting a clear scope and price range, and taking a concrete next step.

## 2. Initial customer hypotheses
Treat these as hypotheses, not established segments:
- Small brands and online sellers needing product visuals.
- Local businesses needing short promotional videos.
- Independent creators needing repeatable visual assets.
- Small marketing teams needing overflow creative production.

Select one segment per validation cycle; do not target all segments at once.

## 3. Core questions
1. What asset is needed, and for what business outcome?
2. How is the need solved today?
3. What is slow, expensive, inconsistent, or difficult?
4. How frequently does the need recur?
5. Who approves the work and controls the budget?
6. What deadline, format, platform, and usage rights are required?
7. What would make the buyer pay for an external service?

## 4. Evidence protocol
Record date, segment, source, exact problem in the buyer's words, existing workaround, urgency, budget signal, requested deliverable, objections, and next action. Separate verbatim evidence from analyst interpretation. Minimize personal data.

- **A:** Paid pilot, deposit, or completed purchase.
- **B:** Written request for proposal with scope, deadline, and budget signal.
- **C:** Specific current problem and credible buying process, but no commitment.
- **D:** General interest, likes, hypothetical answers, or vague compliments.

## 5. Minimum validation gate
Before committing to paid tools:
- Complete at least 8 substantive conversations in one segment.
- Identify at least 3 recurring, specific problems.
- Obtain at least 2 concrete quote requests or equivalent buying signals.
- Attempt to secure 1 paid pilot with written scope.
- Record negative evidence and reasons for rejection.

These are internal decision thresholds, not statistical claims.

## 6. Experiment card
Record hypothesis, target segment, offer/sample, channel/date range, time and cash budget (default Rp0), success metric, stop condition, evidence IDs, result, confidence, and next decision.

## 7. Stop / continue rules
Continue only when evidence becomes specific and credible. Revise the segment or offer if conversations reveal a different urgent problem. Stop a channel after repeated low-quality responses. Do not inflate results with duplicates or vanity metrics.

## 8. Ethics
Use public business contact routes and platform-compliant research. No private-group scraping, access-control bypass, purchased personal data, spam, or unsolicited mass messaging. Respect opt-outs and platform terms.

## 9. Output
Each cycle ends with a one-page decision: **continue / revise / stop**, evidence grade, unresolved assumptions, and the smallest next experiment.
