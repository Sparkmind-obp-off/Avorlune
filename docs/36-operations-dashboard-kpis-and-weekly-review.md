# Operations Dashboard KPIs and Weekly Review

**Status:** Proposed measurement framework; targets must be set after baseline data exists.  
**Purpose:** Measure whether AVORLUNE can acquire, produce, and deliver profitable creative work reliably.

## 1. North Star
**Accepted, rights-cleared deliverables delivered on time with positive contribution margin.** Do not optimize for generation volume alone.

## 2. Funnel metrics
Qualified inquiries by segment/source; brief completion rate; quote requests and quotes sent; quote acceptance rate; paid pilot conversion; time from inquiry to paid start; reasons lost (price, timing, scope, trust, capability, no response).

## 3. Production metrics
On-time delivery; first-pass approval; average revision rounds; actual labor hours vs estimate; direct tool/asset cost per project; defect/rework rate; rights-check completion; client satisfaction only when measured consistently.

## 4. Financial metrics
Revenue collected (not merely quoted); direct project costs; contribution margin (collected revenue minus direct variable costs); effective hourly contribution; refunds/chargebacks/overdue balances; cash spent on subscriptions/tools; outstanding receivables. Keep tax/accounting treatment separate from operational contribution metrics.

## 5. Research quality metrics
Evidence records by grade A–D; independent sources per insight; evidence recency; repeated problem frequency within sampled segment; experiments completed vs planned; decisions changed by evidence.

## 6. Weekly review agenda
1. What was delivered and accepted?
2. What cash was collected and what did each project cost?
3. Which inquiry or segment produced the strongest buying signal?
4. Where did time, revisions, or quality defects accumulate?
5. Are rights, privacy, or security issues unresolved?
6. What should be stopped, simplified, or repeated?
7. What is the single highest-value experiment for next week?

## 7. Data rules
Every metric needs a definition, owner, source, update frequency, and known limitation. Do not mix demo/test records with real customers. Do not publish customer-level data. Mark estimates separately from actuals.

## 8. Initial guardrails
Cash spend stays Rp0 unless the owner explicitly approves a specific amount and purpose. No success target before a baseline. No fabricated case study, conversion rate, revenue, or satisfaction score. Review at least one negative outcome each week.

## 9. Weekly decision record
Date | Segment | Evidence | Delivery/financial result | Risk | Decision | Next experiment | Owner
