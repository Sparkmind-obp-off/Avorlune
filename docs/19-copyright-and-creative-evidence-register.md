# AVORLUNE — Copyright & Creative Evidence Register

**Status:** Internal evidence preparation — no copyright recordation submitted.

## 1. Purpose
Track original works, human creative contribution, ownership, third-party assets and future DJKI recordation decisions. This register does not itself create or certify copyright.

## 2. Legal distinction
DJKI explains that copyright protection for a qualifying work begins when it is created/expressed, not because it is recorded. A recordation letter is initial evidence of ownership, not an absolute adjudication of every ownership dispute. For AI-assisted works, preserve evidence of meaningful human authorship and creative choices; do not assume fully autonomous AI output is eligible for recordation as a human-authored work.

## 3. Evidence package for each work
1. Work ID and title.
2. Work category (artwork, writing, photography, cinematography, software, etc.).
3. Creator(s) and claimed copyright owner.
4. Date first fixed/created and version history.
5. Original editable/source files.
6. Dated drafts, sketches, storyboards, project files and revision history.
7. Human creative contribution statement: concept, composition, selection, arrangement, editing, retouching, direction and final decisions.
8. AI tool/provider and model/version where known.
9. Applicable tool terms and commercial-use license evidence at time of creation.
10. Third-party asset inventory (music, fonts, stock, photos, footage, voice, likeness, templates).
11. Contributor agreements, assignments or licenses.
12. Publication date and URL, if published.
13. Client permissions and restrictions, if commissioned.
14. Decision: retain evidence only / consider recordation / not eligible or not owned.

## 5. AI production provenance checklist
- [ ] Human creative brief exists.
- [ ] Human-authored prompt/creative direction retained.
- [ ] Key generation candidates retained where appropriate.
- [ ] Human selection and arrangement decisions recorded.
- [ ] Human editing, compositing, retouching or post-production files retained.
- [ ] Final work and intermediate versions timestamped.
- [ ] Tool terms and commercial-use permissions saved.
- [ ] Third-party likeness, voice, trademark, music and stock rights cleared.
- [ ] Client contract states ownership/license and portfolio permissions.
- [ ] No confidential client data entered into AI tools without authorization.

Prompt text alone may not establish authorship of every generated output. Assess the actual human contribution to the final work and current DJKI guidance before filing.

## 6. Future DJKI application preparation
DJKI's current procedure page lists, depending on applicant and work type, identity documents for applicant/creator, declaration, sample of the work, transfer evidence where creator and owner differ, entity documents for a legal-entity owner, tax information and publication evidence. Exact attachments and file limits must be checked for the selected work type in the portal on filing day.

Current tariff page lists IDR 200,000 per application for works other than songs/music, and IDR 0 for songs/music under the current tariff schedule. Reconfirm before submitting.
