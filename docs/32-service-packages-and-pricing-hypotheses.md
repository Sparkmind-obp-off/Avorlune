# Service Packages and Pricing Hypotheses

**Status:** Draft for validation; no public prices approved.  
**Purpose:** Define bounded outcomes without promising capabilities or rights that have not been verified.

## 1. Offer architecture
Start with two service lines:
- **AI Image Production:** product, campaign, and brand-supporting still visuals.
- **AI Video Production:** short-form promotional and campaign video assets.

Sell a defined creative outcome, not access to an AI tool or vague unlimited generation.

## 2. Package hypotheses
### Image — Pilot
One creative direction; a small agreed set of final stills; one consolidated revision round; agreed dimensions/formats; one defined campaign use case.

### Image — Campaign
Multiple coordinated visual assets; agreed direction and output list; up to two consolidated revision rounds if stated in the quote; documented schedule and usage license.

### Video — Pilot
One short video concept; agreed duration, aspect ratio, and destination platform; defined scenes/versions; one consolidated revision round. Music, voice, stock, and third-party assets listed separately.

### Video — Campaign
A coordinated set of short-form videos or cutdowns; script/concept approval before production; defined formats, revision rounds, and delivery milestones.

Names, quantities, and prices remain configurable until validated. These are not yet advertised standardized packages.

## 3. Quote formula
Build each quote from creative planning, production time, human editing/compositing/sound/QA, actual tool and asset costs, revision/project-management allowance, commercial usage scope and third-party licenses, contingency, and target margin.

Minimum price must cover expected labor and direct costs. Do not price from competitor screenshots alone.

## 4. Quote must specify
- Deliverable count, duration, dimensions, file format.
- Concept/script and approval checkpoints.
- Included and excluded work.
- Revision definition and feedback deadline.
- Timeline and client dependencies.
- Price, payment milestones, applicable taxes.
- Usage license or assignment wording, territory, term, channels, exclusivity if any.
- Third-party asset and AI-tool limitations.
- Cancellation, refund, and late-feedback handling.

## 5. Pricing experiments
Test one offer at a time. Record quoted price, segment, acceptance/rejection, objections, scope changes, actual hours, direct costs, and realized margin. Do not change price and scope simultaneously unless documented.

## 6. Guardrails
No fake discounts, fabricated testimonials, invented case studies, guaranteed sales/virality, unlimited revisions, or undefined all-rights promises. No production start before scope, payment terms, and required client assets are confirmed. No paid subscription before a real job requires it and the cost is approved.

## 7. Approval gate
The owner must approve the actual price list, package quantities, license terms, and refund policy before public publication.
