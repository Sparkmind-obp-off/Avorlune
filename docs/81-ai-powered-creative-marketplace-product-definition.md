# 81 — AI-Powered Creative Marketplace: Product Definition

**Status:** Approved product direction for product design and implementation.  
**Brand:** AVORLUNE  
**Category:** AI-Powered Creative Production Marketplace  
**Repository:** `Sparkmind-obp-off/Avorlune` · `main`

## 1. Product decision

AVORLUNE is a software platform where customers discover, configure, order and receive AI-generated creative assets through a curated service marketplace.

The customer experience should feel as simple as selecting a service from a freelancer marketplace: choose what they need, review deliverables and price/scope, submit creative inputs, pay, follow progress and receive the finished assets. The fulfilment model is different: AVORLUNE uses approved AI software/providers and its own production orchestration rather than assigning the order to an independent freelancer.

**AVORLUNE sells completed creative outcomes, not access to AI tools.**

## 2. Core promise

"Choose the creative output you need. AVORLUNE produces it with AI-powered workflows and delivers ready-to-use assets."

Primary value:
- Faster path from brief to finished asset than sourcing and coordinating a freelancer for suitable standardized work.
- A clear catalog with defined deliverables and requirements.
- AI-assisted production that can lower marginal production cost.
- Repeatable workflows and consistent output specifications.
- One order, payment and delivery experience.
- Human review and escalation where quality, rights, safety or ambiguity require it.

Do not guarantee that every order is cheaper or faster than every freelancer. Validate actual cost, turnaround and quality by service category.

## 3. Product is software-first

The platform, not a human agency workflow, is the product. The default journey should be self-serve:
1. Browse catalog.
2. Select a creative service/package.
3. Configure output requirements.
4. Upload/reference authorized source material.
5. Preview scope, price and estimated delivery.
6. Sign in and place order.
7. Pay.
8. Follow generation and quality status.
9. Review outputs and use included revision/regen allowance.
10. Download final assets.

Custom/high-complexity requests may enter a quote-required path. Human support is an exception/escalation and quality layer, not the default production mechanism.

## 4. Initial catalog lanes

- AI Video: product ads, UGC-style concepts, short-form social clips, explainers, promo clips.
- AI Image: product visuals, lifestyle images, campaign key visuals, social posts, thumbnails.
- Content Packs: coordinated sets of images/videos/captions for a campaign.
- Creative Adaptation: resize, translate, localize or vary an approved creative for specified channels.
- Custom Creative: quote-required work that does not fit standardized offers.

Only publish a listing when its provider capability, output quality, rights, price/cost model, and fulfillment path have been tested.

## 5. What AVORLUNE is not

- Not an open freelancer marketplace.
- Not a marketplace where customers hire third-party human providers.
- Not a generic prompt shop.
- Not an AI model or GPU provider.
- Not a reseller of provider accounts, subscriptions or credits.
- Not a promise of fully autonomous perfect output.
- Not an advertising agency promising reach, virality or sales outcomes.

## 6. Provider model

External AI providers are internal production dependencies. A provider may be connected through an official API, approved integration or a permitted operator workflow. A provider is not automatically a seller in the customer marketplace. Customers purchase AVORLUNE offers and receive AVORLUNE deliverables.

Provider choice is internal and may vary by output type, quality, latency, cost, rights and availability. Provider names should only be disclosed where useful and permitted by the provider terms.

## 7. Human involvement

Automation-first does not mean zero accountability. Human review is required for:
- unclear or conflicting briefs;
- brand/logo/product fidelity issues;
- rights and likeness concerns;
- sensitive or regulated claims;
- failed or low-confidence generations;
- customer disputes and exceptions;
- final release where the service's risk tier requires approval.

Routine, low-risk, validated services may use automated QA gates with sampling, monitoring and a clear support path.

## 8. Business model

Revenue comes from:
- fixed-price standardized creative offers once unit economics are validated;
- configurable packages and add-ons;
- quote-required custom jobs;
- recurring bundles or content plans after repeat demand is proven.

The customer pays AVORLUNE. The platform pays for internal provider usage and operating costs. There is no freelancer payout or peer-to-peer seller settlement in phase 1.

## 9. North Star

Completed paid creative orders that pass quality and rights gates and are delivered on time, with positive contribution margin and repeat purchase.

Supporting metrics: catalog conversion, order completion, generation success, first-pass acceptance, revision rate, cost per accepted asset, delivery time, gross/contribution margin, refund rate and repeat order rate.
