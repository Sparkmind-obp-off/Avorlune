# 62 — Pricing, Unit Economics and Capacity

**Status:** Commercial model; prices intentionally not set.

## Pricing principle

Price the creative outcome and agreed scope, not a number of Genspark credits. Do not publish invented prices. Fixed prices require validated scope, cost, capacity, and contribution margin.

## Quote cost factors

Deliverable type/quantity, complexity, concepts/variants, duration/resolution/format, source cleanup, AI tool usage allocation, human production and QA time, revisions, deadline/rush capacity, licensed assets, payment fees, taxes/overhead, risk allowance, and target margin.

## Unit economics

Record accepted revenue, provider/tool cost allocation, production labor, licensed assets, payment fees, other direct costs, refunds/credits, and contribution.

Contribution = net revenue − direct provider costs − direct production labor − licensed assets − payment fees − other direct costs.

Do not double-count monthly subscriptions. Document a consistent allocation method and distinguish estimates from actuals.

## Packaging

Define packages by outcomes: number/type of assets, dimensions/duration/formats, variants/channels, creative direction, revision rounds, timeline, and usage rights. Avoid packages defined only as AI credits or prompts.

## Capacity

Before promising a deadline, inspect active queue, human review capacity, provider limits/availability, credit capacity, revision workload, and storage/delivery readiness. Include realistic buffers for AI iteration.

## Promotions and review

Promotions state eligibility, dates, limits, and refund interaction. Do not advertise cashback or guaranteed savings without funding and operational support. Review actual margin and conversion after each cohort; adjust from evidence.
