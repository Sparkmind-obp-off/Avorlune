# Research Scoring & Insight Framework

> **PROPOSAL — not implemented.** No Apify token, connector, migration or paid run exists. Any run requires explicit owner budget approval.

## Opportunity score (0–100)
Score each dimension 0–5, then calculate score / 5 × weight:
- Explicit buying intent: 30%
- Problem recurrence: 20%
- Relevance to AVORLUNE services: 20%
- Evidence diversity: 10%
- Delivery feasibility and margin potential: 10%
- Differentiation potential: 10%

A high score prioritizes an experiment; it does not prove a market.

## Evidence grade
A: explicit public buyer request or verifiable transaction/commission evidence. B: repeated problem statements plus credible commercial activity. C: engagement without clear purchase intent. D: isolated anecdote, viral post or unsupported claim.

## Confidence
High: multiple independent sources, consistent context and low ambiguity. Medium: repeated evidence but limited sample or mixed signals. Low: single source, unclear context or engagement-only pattern.

## Creative pattern coding
Hook: problem, transformation, curiosity, proof, offer, story. Format: product hero, before/after, UGC-style, cinematic montage, tutorial, genuine testimonial, explainer. Visual: macro, studio, lifestyle, motion typography, 3D, surreal, editorial. CTA: inquire, request quote, shop, book, follow, save. Offer: deliverable, turnaround, revisions, usage rights, price visibility. Industry: beauty, fashion, food, property, local services, creator brands, other.

## Decision rules
Build a test brief only when a clear customer problem and feasible service response exist. Do not claim trending from a small/biased sample. Never fabricate testimonials, client results, engagement or conversion. Prefer original AVORLUNE demonstrations over recreating protected expression. Record contradictory evidence and rejected hypotheses.
