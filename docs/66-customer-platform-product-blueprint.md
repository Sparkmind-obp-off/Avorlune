# 66 — Customer Platform Product Blueprint

**Status:** Target specification; implementation pending.

AVORLUNE becomes a service-led AI Creative Production platform with a customer portal, customer CRM and a safe demonstration journey. AVORLUNE itself fulfils work using authorized creative tools such as Genspark plus human creative direction, editing, QA and delivery. This is not an open freelancer marketplace.

## Customer journey
Public browse → optional sign-in → optional onboarding → choose service → Ready-to-order configuration or Custom Project brief → AVORLUNE qualification → quotation → accept scope → 50% deposit → production → preview/revisions → 50% balance → final QA/provenance → private delivery → support/reorder.

Public browsing remains open. Customer authentication is required before submitting a brief or viewing private project data. Preserve the brief when redirecting to sign-in. Onboarding can be skipped and customers may go directly to the catalog.

## Customer workspace
Overview, service discovery, saved briefs, projects, quote versions, payment milestones, production timeline, previews/feedback, private deliverables, support tickets and profile/preferences.

## CRM
One customer 360 view links verified identity, inquiries, projects, quotes, payment references, support, consent and activity. Search by customer ID, verified email, project reference, status, service and date. Internal notes remain separate from customer-visible messages. Paginate results and bound exports.

## Demo
Public demo uses synthetic customer/project data, sample assets and simulated quote/payment/production states. It never creates real invoices, payments, provider calls, production jobs, customer notifications or production D1 writes.

## Commercial invariants
All paid services: 50% deposit before production and 50% balance before final delivery. A free brief submission is not an order, invoice or production commitment. Final delivery requires verified balance, human QA, provenance/license checks and authorization.

## Success measures
Real brief completion, quote acceptance, deposit conversion, turnaround, revision rate, final approval, repeat orders and support response. Exclude demo traffic.

## Non-goals
No freelancer bidding, seller accounts, public customer directory, credit resale, public private-asset URLs or unreviewed autonomous delivery.
