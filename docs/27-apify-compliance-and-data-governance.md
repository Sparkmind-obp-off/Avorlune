# Apify Compliance & Data Governance

> **PROPOSAL — not implemented.** No Apify token, connector, migration or paid run exists. Any run requires explicit owner budget approval.

Status: operational policy draft; obtain legal review where needed.

## Allowed posture
Use public pages and data accessible lawfully and consistently with platform terms. Respect robots/access controls, rate limits, copyright, privacy and applicable Indonesian law. Use Actors only within published terms and platform rules. Keep source URLs and brief factual observations; avoid downloading or republishing media.

## Not allowed
Private accounts, private messages, personal contact details or sensitive personal data; credentials, cookies, session tokens or OTPs; bypassing login walls, paywalls, CAPTCHAs or technical access controls; automated engagement, spam, unsolicited outreach or posting; copying competitors' complete creative assets, scripts or distinctive protected expression.

## Data minimization
Do not collect or infer sensitive traits. Avoid storing personal names where a public business/creator handle is enough. Do not profile individuals. Delete accidental personal data and record the incident.

## Copyright
A public post is not a free-to-use asset. Research outputs should be links, short factual notes and original analysis. Any source media used in portfolio, client deliverable or public post requires a separate rights basis/license or an independently created original asset.

## Vendor review
Before production use, review developer identity, pricing, data handling, retention, terms, platform compliance, reliability and support.

## Incident response
Stop affected runs, preserve minimal audit evidence, restrict access, assess exposure, delete unauthorized copies and seek appropriate legal advice where needed.
