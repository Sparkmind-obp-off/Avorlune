# 63 — Customer Policy and Contract Requirements

**Status:** Requirements for legal drafting, not legal advice. Existing legal pages remain DRAFT until owner and qualified Indonesian counsel review.

## Required documents

Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Service Scope/Quotation Terms, Cancellation and Refund Policy, AI-Assisted Production and Licensing Disclosure, Customer Source Material/Rights Declaration, Support and Complaint Procedure.

## Contract and scope

A brief is an inquiry, not an accepted order. The order/contract forms only under reviewed terms when the customer accepts a valid quotation and required conditions are met. Accepted quote identifies deliverables, price, timing, revisions, payment schedule, usage rights, and exclusions.

## 50/50 payment disclosure

For custom projects disclose:
- 50% deposit after quote acceptance and before production.
- Remaining 50% after preview/revisions and before final file release.
- Production starts only after deposit verification.
- Final unwatermarked deliverables release only after balance verification.
- Manual payment/provider downtime process.
- Taxes, fees, or additional charges before acceptance.

Do not say deposits are automatically non-refundable. Cancellation/refund outcomes depend on accepted terms, work performed, costs properly incurred, applicable law, and circumstances.

## Cancellation/refund topics

Address cancellation before/after production start, after preview, provider failure, missed delivery commitments, scope changes/customer delay, duplicate/incorrect payment, partial delivery, refund method/timing, disputes, and complaint escalation.

Refunds are auditable and not marked complete until money movement is evidenced.

## AI/IP disclosure

Explain AI-assisted production with human direction, editing, selection, and QA. Do not promise exclusive copyright in purely AI-generated portions. Distinguish human-authored work, customer materials, provider outputs, and third-party licensed assets. Commercial use depends on applicable provider plan/terms and third-party licenses at generation/delivery time.

Do not imply Genspark credits/accounts are transferred or resold.

## Customer materials and portfolio

Customer confirms authorization for supplied logos, images, footage, trademarks, likenesses, music, and other materials. AVORLUNE still performs reasonable risk review and may reject unlawful requests.

Portfolio use requires separate explicit permission; do not bundle it as a service condition.

## Privacy

Define purposes, data categories, retention, processors/providers, cross-border processing where relevant, security, deletion requests, and customer rights. Avoid uploading unnecessary confidential/sensitive data to AI providers.

## Indonesian legal review

Counsel should review consumer protection, electronic transactions, privacy/electronic systems, contract formation/e-consent, deposit/refund terms, tax/invoices, payment-provider/merchant rules, copyright/trademark/personality rights, AI disclosure, and data processing/transfers.

Remove DRAFT only after owner approval, counsel review, operational consistency, correct version/date/contact, and recording the policy version accepted by customer.
