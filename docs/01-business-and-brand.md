# 01 — Business & Brand Foundation

## 1. Brand definition
**Name:** AVORLUNE (working name; not legally cleared)  
**Category:** AI Creative Production  
**What it is:** a creative production service that uses AI tools and human direction to produce campaign-ready visual content.

**What it is not**
- Not an AI foundation-model company.
- Not a generic chatbot or AI SaaS wrapper.
- Not an automated content-spam or mass-posting service.
- Not a promise that every output is fully autonomous or instantly perfect.

## 2. Positioning
AVORLUNE helps brands and creators turn a clear idea into polished visual content through a guided production process: brief → creative direction → generation → human review → revision → delivery.

**Positioning statement:** For small businesses, growing brands and creators who need distinctive visual content, AVORLUNE is an AI-assisted creative production partner that combines fast experimentation with human creative judgment.

## 3. Initial customer segments
1. Small businesses that need product visuals and social content.
2. E-commerce sellers that need consistent product/campaign imagery.
3. Creators and personal brands that need short-form visual assets.
4. Marketing teams that need concept exploration and campaign variations.

Start with one narrow segment during validation; do not build separate product experiences for every segment.

## 4. Initial service catalogue
### AI Image Production
- Product/lifestyle image concepts
- Campaign key visuals
- Social-media image sets
- Creative variations and resizing (where included in scope)

### AI Video Production
- Short-form promotional videos
- Product/brand visual clips
- Story-led campaign concepts
- Motion-led edits assembled from generated assets (subject to tool capability)

Every offer must specify deliverable count, format, aspect ratio, duration (for video), revision allowance, turnaround estimate, source-material requirements and usage/licensing terms.

## 5. Offer design
Use quote-based packages initially rather than inventing fixed market prices:
- **Starter:** one tightly scoped creative request.
- **Campaign:** a coordinated set of image/video deliverables.
- **Ongoing:** recurring monthly production capacity after repeat demand is demonstrated.

Price each job using production time, generation/tool cost, editing, revision risk, licensing and margin. Publish prices only after cost and demand validation.

## 6. Customer promise
- Clear brief and scope before production.
- Human quality review before delivery.
- Transparent status and revision limits.
- Honest disclosure of AI-assisted production when relevant.
- No guaranteed outcomes such as sales, virality or ad performance.

## 7. Brand experience
**Visual direction:** premium, clean, sophisticated, modern, cinematic/editorial.  
**Voice:** confident, clear, collaborative; avoid exaggerated AI claims.  
**Design cues:** strong typography, generous whitespace, restrained palette, image-led layouts, concise service cards and clear calls to action.

## 8. Validation before scale
Interview or observe prospective buyers; publish sample work; test one paid pilot offer; record objections, willingness to pay, revision load and repeat intent. Do not infer demand from likes or views alone.

## 9. Brand and legal gate
Before public launch, verify exact and similar marks, domains and social handles directly. Search PDKI and WIPO including phonetic variants and relevant classes with qualified IP advice. This document does not establish trademark clearance or domain availability.
