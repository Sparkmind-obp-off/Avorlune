# 10 — Roadmap

Roadmap is gated by evidence, not calendar promises.

## Phase 0 — Foundation
- Confirm brand screening and naming decision.
- Select first customer segment and one paid pilot offer.
- Produce 3–5 representative sample projects with rights-cleared assets.
- Define scope, revision and usage terms.
**Exit gate:** clear offer and evidence of qualified buyer interest.

## Phase 1 — Concierge validation
- Run inquiries through a simple landing page/form.
- Scope and deliver initial paid pilots manually.
- Track costs, time, feedback, approvals and repeat intent.
**Exit gate:** several completed paid projects and known unit economics.

## Phase 2 — Portal MVP
- Public website and portfolio.
- Structured inquiry form.
- Admin project inbox and status workflow.
- Private asset delivery and feedback.
**Exit gate:** customer can complete the full brief-to-delivery journey through the portal.

## Phase 3 — Operational automation
- Notifications.
- Signed upload/download workflows.
- Quote acceptance and manual payment confirmation.
- Production templates and reporting.
**Exit gate:** reduced admin overhead without quality degradation.

## Phase 4 — Selective AI integrations
- Verify one provider's official API and commercial terms.
- Build one adapter for a specific production bottleneck.
- Add cost and usage tracking.
**Exit gate:** measurable improvement in speed, cost or quality.

## Phase 5 — Scale what repeats
- Recurring production packages.
- Customer accounts and project history.
- Payment gateway integration after credentials and settlement tests.
- Queue-based jobs if workload requires.
**Exit gate:** repeat demand and stable delivery economics.

## Non-goals
Do not build a multi-provider autonomous AI platform, marketplace, subscription wallet or large orchestration engine before paid usage validates the need.
