# AVORLUNE — Client Rights, NDA & Asset Licensing Checklist

**Status:** Contract preparation checklist — not a signed agreement and not legal advice.

## 1. Before accepting a project
- [ ] Identify the contracting party and authorized contact.
- [ ] Define deliverables, format, quantity, duration, resolution and platform.
- [ ] Define milestones, revision count, acceptance criteria and delivery method.
- [ ] Confirm whether the client supplies source materials.
- [ ] Obtain client confirmation that supplied logos, footage, photos, music, scripts, likenesses and other assets may lawfully be used.
- [ ] Identify sensitive/confidential materials and permitted AI tools.
- [ ] Decide whether work may be shown in AVORLUNE portfolio/case studies.
- [ ] Decide whether final work is licensed or assigned, and when rights transfer.
- [ ] Set payment and cancellation terms.

## 2. Rights model to choose per project
Do not promise full copyright ownership by default. Choose and explain one model:
- **License to client:** AVORLUNE retains rights it owns and grants a defined license (scope, territory, duration, media, exclusivity).
- **Assignment/transfer:** transfer only rights AVORLUNE actually owns and can legally transfer, documented in writing and effective at a defined time.
- **Client-owned materials:** client retains rights in supplied materials; AVORLUNE receives limited permission to use them for the project.
- **Third-party/tool assets:** remain subject to their own provider terms and licenses; they may not be transferable or exclusive.

For AI-assisted work, explain that provider terms and applicable law may limit exclusivity or ownership claims. Never guarantee exclusive copyright in an output where rights are uncertain.

## 3. Essential clauses for a client agreement
1. Parties and authority.
2. Scope of services and deliverables.
3. Fees, payment schedule, late payment and cancellation.
4. Revision rounds and change requests.
5. Client materials and warranties of permission.
6. AI-assisted production disclosure where appropriate.
7. Ownership and license for final deliverables.
8. Excluded materials, third-party assets and provider terms.
9. Portfolio/publicity permission as explicit opt-in.
10. Confidentiality and permitted disclosures.
11. Personal data and likeness/voice permissions.
12. Acceptance, delivery and retention period.
13. Warranties, liability limits and indemnities, drafted proportionately.
14. Dispute resolution and governing law.
15. Signatures and effective date.

## 4. NDA / confidentiality controls
- Define confidential information clearly.
- Exclude information already public, independently developed or lawfully obtained without confidentiality duty.
- Limit use to the stated project purpose.
- Limit access to people who need to know and are bound by confidentiality.
- Specify security measures and incident notification.
- Define return/deletion on request or project end, subject to lawful retention.
- Define duration and survival of confidentiality obligations.
- Do not use an NDA as a substitute for a proper IP assignment or license.

## 5. Third-party asset clearance register

| Asset type | Evidence to retain | Key risk |
|---|---|---|
| AI generation tool | Terms, plan, model, date, commercial-use permission | Terms vary by plan and may change |
| Music/audio | License, receipt, territory/platform scope | Platform-only license may not cover client ads |
| Font | Commercial license and embedding rights | Desktop/web/app rights differ |
| Stock photo/video | License and permitted use | Editorial-only or restricted use |
| Voice/likeness | Written consent and usage scope | Privacy, personality and publicity rights |
| Logo/trademark | Client authorization | Avoid implying endorsement or ownership |
| Templates/plugins | License and redistribution limits | Source template may not be transferable |

## 6. AI provider review before production
For every tool, record provider/product, account tier, terms URL and review date, commercial-use permission, output rights, prohibited content, data retention/training policy, client-data restrictions, attribution requirements, and API/automation/resale permissions.

Do not assume web UI terms authorize API, automated, resale or white-label use.

## 7. Internal rule
No client deliverable is published in a public portfolio, used as a case study, shared with another client, or used to train/customize a model unless the contract and permissions explicitly allow it.

## 8. Legal review trigger
Obtain qualified Indonesian legal review before using the final agreement for high-value, exclusive, regulated, cross-border or materially sensitive projects.
