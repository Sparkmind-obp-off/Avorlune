# 68 — Service Discovery & Brief Builder UX

Public discovery works without an account. The premium interface stays clean, calm, cinematic and mobile-first.

## Navigation
Public: Home, Services, Work (only genuine approved work), How it works, About, Contact, Sign in, Get started. No admin link or admin sign-in affordance.

Customer: Overview, Explore services, My projects, Quotes, Payments, Deliverables, Support, Profile.

## Onboarding
Welcome and explain human-reviewed AI creative production. Let the customer select image, video, campaign/mixed or not sure; optional customer context; optional organization; then finish or skip to catalog. Do not demand phone, budget or company size without a service-specific reason.

## Service catalog
Categories: AI Image Production, AI Video Production, Campaign & Social Creative. Service detail states use case, deliverables, required inputs, formats, revisions, exclusions, timing assumptions, usage/license notes and pricing mode. Fixed prices appear only after validation; otherwise use quotation.

## Brief builder
Progressive steps: objective, service/configuration, audience/use, creative direction/references, deliverables/quantity/formats, source uploads, rights declaration, review and submit. Save/resume for authenticated users. Server validates every field and upload. Explain next steps and expected response process.

## Authentication prompts
Ask for sign-in when saving across devices, submitting, or opening private workspace. Preserve entered data. Do not force account creation just to browse.

## Purchase paths
Ready-to-order and Custom Project both use the same 50/50 payment policy. Do not imply a fixed price if scope requires quotation.

## Demo
Demo journey is visibly labelled and fully simulated. No live order, payment, production or notification is created.
