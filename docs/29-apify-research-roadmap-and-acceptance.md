## Phase 4 — recurring intelligence
- [ ] Schedule research only after value and cost are proven.

Acceptance: recurring research creates measurable business value within approved budget.

## North Star
Evidence-backed creative experiments per month that produce qualified inquiries or paid work. Dataset size, scrape volume and views are not North Star metrics.

## Definition of done
Preparation documents exist, scope and limits are clear, and no paid scraping or production schema change has occurred.
