# AVORLUNE — Brand Identity & Asset Production Brief

**Status:** Creative direction draft — not final brand approval.  
**Brand category:** AI Creative Production.

## 1. Positioning
AVORLUNE is a premium AI-assisted creative production studio focused initially on image and video production for brands, creators and businesses. It is not positioned as an AI model provider or generic AI SaaS.

## 2. Brand attributes
- Premium, restrained, contemporary.
- Cinematic and editorial.
- Clean composition and confident negative space.
- Sophisticated without looking cold or overly futuristic.
- Human creative direction supported by AI production tools.

## 3. Visual system — working direction
- Primary palette: deep ink/near-black, warm ivory, muted silver.
- Accent: restrained cool lavender or pale champagne.
- Typography: modern high-contrast display serif paired with neutral sans-serif.
- Layout: editorial grid, generous whitespace, strong image-led compositions.
- Avoid generic robot heads, glowing circuit brains, excessive gradients and stock-AI clichés.

## 4. Logo concept routes
1. **Wordmark:** AVORLUNE in refined custom uppercase letterforms.
2. **Monogram:** abstract A/V/L geometry suitable for avatar and watermark.
3. **Celestial/editorial symbol:** subtle orbital or light-arc motif, avoiding obvious moon/star clip-art.

The SVG in assets/brand/avorlune-logo-concept.svg is an initial editable direction only. It is not trademark-cleared or approved as the final logo.

## 6. Asset production and rights
- Keep source files and dated versions.
- Record human creative brief, design decisions, edits and approvals.
- Record fonts, stock elements, plugins and AI tools used.
- Confirm commercial use and redistribution rights before public/client use.
- Do not state AI-generated artwork is automatically copyrightable or exclusive.
- Conduct trademark similarity search before treating a symbol or wordmark as safe to use.

## 7. Approval gate
No asset becomes the official AVORLUNE identity until the owner explicitly approves it after visual review and preliminary clearance. Until then, use “concept”, “draft” or “proposed”.
