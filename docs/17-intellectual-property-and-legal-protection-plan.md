## 3. IP categories and relevance

| Regime | AVORLUNE relevance | Current action |
|---|---|---|
| Trademark | Brand name, logo and potentially distinctive slogan | Prepare clearance and dossier; word mark first |
| Copyright | Human-authored logo artwork, scripts, storyboards, photography, audiovisual works, illustrations, software and documentation | Preserve evidence; selectively record important eligible works later |
| Trade secret | Confidential workflows, pricing logic, client lists, prompt systems, production SOPs and know-how | Protect now through access controls and confidentiality terms |
| Patent / simple patent | Qualifying new technical invention or process | No current filing planned |
| Industrial design | New product appearance meeting novelty requirements | No current filing planned |
| DTLST | Integrated-circuit layout design | Not relevant now |
| Geographical indication | Qualifying geographically linked product/indication | Not relevant now |
| Plant variety protection | New plant varieties | Not relevant now |
| Related rights | Sound recordings, performances or broadcasts where AVORLUNE owns qualifying subject matter | Assess per project and contract |

DJKI's portal lists trademark, patent, copyright, industrial design, geographical indication, DTLST and trade secret services. Plant variety protection has its own legal regime and is not a likely AVORLUNE asset.

## 4. Filing priorities

### Priority 1 — AVORLUNE word mark
- Search exact and similar marks in PDKI.
- Search WIPO Global Brand Database as a supplementary check.
- Search spelling, phonetic, visual and conceptual similarities in related services.
- Prepare applicant identity and precise service specification.
- Evaluate Nice classes 35 and 41 first based on actual services.
- Consider class 42 only if AVORLUNE itself offers software, SaaS or technology services.
- Consider class 9 only if it sells downloadable software or qualifying goods.
- Obtain current classification and professional review before payment.

### Priority 2 — AVORLUNE logo mark
Finalize and approve the logo before filing. Keep monochrome/color variants, vector source, dated drafts and design rationale. Search visually similar marks. Decide whether a separate logo application is commercially justified.

### Priority 3 — Copyright recordation
Select original, important works with documented human creative contribution. Candidates may include final logo artwork, brand guide, original scripts/storyboards, photography, audiovisual works, software and original documentation. Do not automatically file every AI output. Preserve human decisions, drafts, editing history, source files, provider terms and asset licenses.

Copyright protection may arise automatically when a qualifying work is expressed in tangible form; recordation is not the condition that creates copyright. AI-only output without meaningful human creative contribution should not be assumed eligible for recordation.

### Priority 4 — Trade secret controls
- Identify confidential information and label it.
- Limit access to confidential files.
- Keep valuable prompt/workflow libraries private.
- Use NDA and confidentiality clauses with contractors, collaborators and vendors.
- Do not publish client confidential material or internal methods without authorization.
- Keep access logs and secure backups where practical.

## 5. Ownership decision — unresolved
Before filing, choose the legal applicant/owner for each asset: individual or relevant legal entity. Do not assume a company automatically owns work created by a founder, freelancer or contractor. Document creator, first owner, transfer or license, and permitted uses. Obtain legal/accounting advice before assigning a brand or portfolio of IP to a company.

## 6. Budget framework
DJKI tariff pages currently list:
- Trademark: IDR 500,000 per class for eligible micro/small applicants; IDR 2,800,000 per class for general applicants.
- Copyright recordation: IDR 200,000 per application for works other than songs/music; IDR 0 for songs/music under the listed tariff.
- Other regimes have separate fees and eligibility requirements.

Reconfirm fees and eligibility on filing day. The IDR 2,000,000 reserve is not guaranteed to cover all combinations of mark, classes, logo and copyright recordation.
