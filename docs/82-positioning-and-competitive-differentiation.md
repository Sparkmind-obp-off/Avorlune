# 82 — Positioning and Competitive Differentiation

**Status:** Approved positioning direction.

## Positioning statement

AVORLUNE is an AI-powered creative production marketplace for businesses, creators and brands that want ready-to-use video and visual content without sourcing, briefing and coordinating a freelancer for every routine creative task.

**Customer shorthand:** "Choose the creative service. Submit your product and brief. AVORLUNE generates and delivers the assets."

## Competitive frame

| Alternative | Customer does | AVORLUNE difference |
|---|---|---|
| Freelancer marketplace | Searches profiles, compares portfolios, briefs a person, negotiates and manages delivery | Curated catalog, structured inputs, standardized workflow and AI-assisted fulfillment |
| Independent freelancer | Coordinates scope, availability, revisions and payment directly | Productized offers, predictable scope, centralized order tracking and repeatable workflows |
| Canva / CapCut | Operates the creative software and makes the content themselves | Customer buys a completed output through a guided order flow, not another editing workspace |
| Standalone AI generator | Chooses tools, writes prompts, iterates, edits and checks outputs | AVORLUNE packages the workflow around a specific customer outcome and deliverable |
| Traditional agency | Buys a broader human-led service with potentially higher overhead | AI-first production for defined, repeatable work; custom human-led work is not the default |

Do not claim that competitors cannot generate video or images. Their generation and editing capabilities are not the point of differentiation.

## Differentiators to prove

1. **Ready-to-order catalog:** customers know what they can buy, what inputs are needed and what they will receive.
2. **Less coordination:** no freelancer search, bidding or repeated manual briefing for standardized services.
3. **AI-first fulfillment:** provider workflows produce assets; AVORLUNE coordinates generation, variants, QA and delivery.
4. **Transparent scope:** quantity, format, duration, aspect ratio, included revisions and delivery assumptions are visible before payment.
5. **Consistent order experience:** payment, status, feedback, revisions and files live in one workspace.
6. **Competitive economics:** AI automation can reduce production cost for suitable work; actual savings must be measured and reflected in prices.
7. **Provider flexibility:** internal provider adapters allow the platform to select suitable tools without forcing customers to learn each tool.

## Messaging hierarchy

Primary: **AI-powered creative production, ready to order.**

Supporting:
- Video and visual assets without the freelancer search.
- Choose a service, submit a brief, receive finished creative assets.
- AI-powered production with quality checks.
- Clear deliverables and transparent order progress.

Avoid:
- "Cheapest video on the internet."
- "Replace every freelancer."
- "One-click perfect video."
- "Guaranteed viral content or sales."
- Claims of specific provider integrations until verified.

## Price positioning

The intended position is accessible and competitive relative to comparable freelancer production for clearly scoped jobs. Do not compare against the cheapest freelancer or imply a universal percentage saving. Build price comparisons using equivalent deliverables, rights, quality, revision scope, turnaround and taxes. Reassess by category.

## Proof required before public claims

For each launch offer, retain:
- benchmark freelancer/agency quote range from a documented sample;
- AVORLUNE provider cost and generation failure rate;
- human review minutes;
- total cost per accepted deliverable;
- median and 90th percentile turnaround;
- first-pass acceptance and revision rate;
- customer willingness-to-pay and repeat intent.

Public claims must match this evidence and have a date and scope.
