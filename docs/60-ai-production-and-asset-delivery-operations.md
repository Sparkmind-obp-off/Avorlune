# 60 — AI Production and Asset Delivery Operations

**Status:** Operating specification.

## Production principle

AVORLUNE fulfils creative service orders with AI-assisted tools and human creative direction. It does not sell provider account access or resell credits. Genspark is the initial human-operated tool. Do not assume an API, unattended automation, commercial rights, or specific feature without checking the active plan and current provider terms.

## Production stages

1. Deposit verified.
2. Project queued and accepted brief locked.
3. Source material and rights reviewed.
4. Creative direction/storyboard prepared.
5. Authorized AI-assisted generation.
6. Selection, refinement, editing, and composition.
7. Internal human quality review.
8. Protected preview created.
9. Customer review and included revisions.
10. Final QA and licensing review.
11. Balance verified.
12. Private final delivery.
13. Archive and cost reconciliation.

## Human quality gate

Review match to brief, brand/product accuracy, text/spelling, visual defects, dimensions/duration/format, audio/music rights, third-party trademark/likeness/copyright risks, provider plan eligibility, and usage restrictions. AI output is not automatically a deliverable.

## Provider and credit governance

Record provider/product, account owner, plan, active dates, commercial-use evidence and terms version, usage allocation, operator/project, restrictions, invoice reference, and review date. Never store passwords, cookies, OTPs, or API keys in project records.

Credit use is an internal cost metric, not a customer balance. Never transfer or resell credits.

## Asset provenance

Record asset ID/project, type (customer source, reference, generated draft, preview, final, licensed stock), operator/time, provider/plan evidence, source IDs, human edits/approvals, license status, permitted use, customer approval, delivery state, and checksum where practical.

## Preview and final

Preview may be watermarked or reduced-resolution and is for review only. Final files require human QA, complete license/provenance, verified balance, authorized approval, private storage, logged access, and a delivery manifest.

If R2 is not provisioned, use approved secure manual delivery in docs/41 and docs/44. Never use unrestricted public object URLs.

## Revisions and costs

Track revision rounds and scope relation. Out-of-scope work becomes a change request under docs/58. Record actual provider cost allocation, production effort, licensed assets, payment fees, and other direct costs. Label estimates separately from actuals.

## Provider incident

If Genspark is unavailable, pause, assess equivalent permitted alternatives, verify terms and quality, seek customer approval for material substitution, and update the delivery estimate. Do not silently send customer material to another provider.

## Acceptance

No production before deposit; every final asset has provenance/license evidence; human QA is mandatory; no final delivery before balance; all files are private and access-controlled.
