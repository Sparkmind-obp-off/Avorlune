# Client Delivery, Support, and Retention Policy

**Status:** Draft internal policy; align with final contract and privacy notice before public use.  
**Purpose:** Define a predictable handoff and avoid indefinite retention of client materials.

## 1. Delivery
Each delivery should include project reference/date, final filenames and formats, dimensions/duration/versions, included items, usage license reference, third-party restrictions, known limitations, approval record, and outstanding balance status.

Use a private, access-controlled delivery channel. Do not expose customer assets through public URLs or guessable identifiers. If secure file storage is not provisioned, agree a temporary approved transfer method; do not represent an unconfigured portal as secure storage.

## 2. Client approval
Record approver, timestamp, approved version, and stated conditions. Approval of creative output does not transfer rights beyond the written license or assignment.

## 3. Support
Support covers delivery access and defects against the agreed brief. New concepts, formats, variants, and changes after approval are new scope unless the agreement says otherwise. Publish support hours and response targets only after capacity is confirmed.

## 4. Retention schedule — proposed
This is a starting policy, not a legally validated schedule:
- Unsuccessful inquiry data: delete or anonymize after 90 days unless there is a documented reason to retain longer.
- Working drafts and temporary exports: delete within 30 days after final acceptance unless requested otherwise or a dispute requires preservation.
- Final deliverables: retain up to 90 days after delivery only if storage/access controls are operational; clients should keep their own copies.
- Contract, invoice, payment, and accounting records: retain for periods required by applicable law and professional advice.
- Security and audit logs: retain only as long as needed for security, disputes, and legal obligations.

Reconcile these periods with Indonesian privacy, tax, consumer, and contract requirements before adoption.

## 5. Deletion and access requests
Verify the requester is authorized. Identify project assets and backups, explain records that must be retained by law or dispute hold, then delete or restrict eligible copies. Record completion without retaining unnecessary content.

## 6. Security controls
Least-privilege access; unique project IDs; no personal details in public links; expiring/revocable links where supported; encryption in transit/at rest where provided; no secrets in Git/prompts/screenshots/client files; revoke access when staff/contractors leave; test restore and deletion procedures.

## 7. Public portfolio use
No client work, brand name, testimonial, or behind-the-scenes material may be published without separate, explicit written permission. Payment is not portfolio consent.
