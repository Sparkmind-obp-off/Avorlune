# Apify Research Strategy — AVORLUNE

Status: PREPARATION / NO SCRAPING RUNS
Purpose: evidence-led market, creative-format and competitor research for AI Creative Production.

## Objectives
1. Identify recurring buyer problems and demand signals for AI image/video production.
2. Map competitor positioning, offer structure, proof, creative style and calls to action.
3. Detect repeatable content formats, hooks, visual patterns and platform conventions.
4. Convert evidence into service packages, portfolio experiments and content briefs.
5. Measure whether research leads to qualified inquiries and paid projects.

## Initial scope
Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube. Geography: Indonesia first; global examples are creative references, not assumed local demand. Categories: AI product ads, fashion/beauty visuals, food and beverage, local business promos, UGC-style ads, cinematic brand films and explainers. Research only public posts/videos and public metadata.

## Research questions
- What outcomes are buyers asking for?
- Which industries repeatedly publish or request short-form visual content?
- Which formats are used by credible studios and creators?
- What hooks, durations, aspect ratios, CTAs and offer promises recur?
- What evidence supports demand: explicit quote requests, repeated purchase intent, active offers and recurring pain points?
- What is differentiated versus generic AI output?

## Method
1. Define one narrow question and sampling window.
2. Search public pages and platform-native public content.
3. Record source URL, retrieval date, platform, creator/business label, post date and observable metrics.
4. Separate facts, interpretations and hypotheses.
5. Code each item by industry, format, hook, visual style, offer, audience and evidence strength.
6. Deduplicate, score and summarize patterns.
7. Convert strongest patterns into one low-cost creative experiment.
8. Record outcomes; do not call a trend validated until an experiment or customer signal supports it.

## Evidence hierarchy
A: direct buyer request, public quote request, paid offer/transaction evidence or repeated explicit purchase intent. B: repeated problem statements and consistent commercial activity. C: engagement without clear buying intent. D: isolated viral content or unverified claims. Views and likes alone are not proof of demand.

## Sampling and outputs
Pilot: 10–20 public items per platform for one narrow question. Expand only if useful and budget-approved. Prefer samples across industries, creator sizes and formats, not only viral posts. Outputs: weekly evidence log, competitor matrix, creative pattern library, opportunity hypotheses, experiment briefs and results.

## Guardrails
Default budget USD 0 / Rp0. No paid Actor runs without explicit spend approval. No bypassing access controls, login walls or platform restrictions. Respect platform terms, law, privacy and copyright. Store only fields needed. Do not republish scraped media; use links, short factual notes and original analysis.
